The Republic of PC Gamers is a concept-grade PC discovery and building experience. It turns a pile of specifications into something you can actually explore.

01

BUILD

Choose a CPU, GPU, motherboard, memory, storage, power supply and cooling. The build engine keeps the configuration understandable and flags common compatibility conflicts.

02

COMPARE

Performance cards show the metrics that matter: estimated gaming FPS, VRAM, power draw, generation and platform details.

03

EVOLVE

The catalog is data-driven. Add future components, older hardware or new generations by editing one data file rather than rebuilding the UI.

PERFORMANCE NUMBERS ARE ESTIMATES FOR THE DEMO EXPERIENCE, NOT BENCHMARK RESULTS. REAL FPS VARIES WITH GAME VERSION, DRIVERS, SETTINGS, CPU/GPU COMBINATION AND SYSTEM CONFIGURATION.
